FAQ

Is this a recruitment service?

No. The current pilot provides career-preparation guidance only. It does not forward profiles to employers or arrange interviews.

How is the career strategy delivered?

In writing, by email. No call is required.

How is my personal information handled?

Form submissions are used only to review and reply to your request. They are sent through Formspree, which may process data outside Korea. K-Talent Bridge deletes pilot submission data within 90 days after the interaction ends, and you can request earlier deletion by email.
